## Participant setup

1. Open your administrator-issued invitation and use its exact invited email address. Either create a separate portal password, follow the verification email and sign in, or choose **Accept with Google** using that verified address. An expired or revoked invitation needs a replacement from the administrator. Password recovery is available from **Forgot password?** after a password has been added.
2. To add Google to a password account, sign in freshly with your portal password, open **Account access** and choose **Link Google to this account**. To add a password to a Google-created account, sign in freshly with Google and choose **Add portal password**. Finish within five minutes; otherwise sign in again and restart. Matching email addresses alone do not link identities. Both methods must return to the same account and retain its configuration. See [Google and linking](hosted-google.md).
3. Open **AimHarder connection**, enter your AimHarder credentials directly in the portal and choose **Connect AimHarder**. Keep these credentials out of AI conversations and MCP/client configuration. The portal and AimHarder email addresses may differ. Confirm the verified connection and membership; if several gyms exist, choose an accessible default and confirm the gym's time zone when required. An assumed zone remains labelled as assumed. See [connection settings](hosted-aimharder-connection.md).
4. Open **Connect ChatGPT or Hermes** and follow the [client authorization instructions](hosted-mcp-authorization.md#chatgpt-connection). Each independently revocable installation uses its own operator-registered public PKCE client. No client secret, portal password or AimHarder password belongs in these settings. Complete your own sign-in and consent. Exact callbacks belong to the selected environment; do not copy authorization links or codes into a conversation.
5. Query your account/gym context and compare the returned gym, time zone and source limits before asking other questions. Occupancy is not attendance; activity counts are entries; incomplete booking history or an absent upcoming-booking match does not prove an exhaustive absence.

For booking or activity actions, first obtain the exact preparation preview. Confirm that specific action in a separate message only after reviewing its gym, target, local date/time and consequences. Late credit loss needs its own explicit confirmation. An available tool, general test approval or client tool auto-approval is not that confirmation. Check the fresh own-account read-back after the attempt; an HTTP success alone is not evidence of the requested state. Uncertain or possibly sent actions must not be automatically replayed.

## Access and lifecycle controls

**Connected MCP clients** revokes only the selected client. **AimHarder connection** can replace the verified upstream account, update its password or disconnect it. Replacement and password updates preserve portal identity and MCP grants while discarding earlier upstream sessions and prepared actions. Disconnection removes the upstream connection; it does not cancel bookings or delete sporting activity. **Account access** provides account deletion, which removes service access and active associated service data. See [connection lifecycle](hosted-aimharder-connection.md#connection-lifecycle-synthetic-evidence-2026-10-19) and [deletion/recovery](hosted-account-deletion.md).

Administrators manage invitations, view participant role/status/connection presence, suspend/reactivate other participants and configure the two independent **Activity access by role** controls. Publication and deletion initially allow `admin` and deny `user`. Booking creation/cancellation and queries have no feature switches. Administration exposes no role editor, participant credentials, sporting history, impersonation or diagnostic-message panel. Suspension retains configuration but denies portal/MCP access; reactivation requires fresh authorization for each MCP client. See [participant administration](hosted-participants.md).

Use a disposable invited participant for destructive lifecycle acceptance. Do not delete the only administrator, remove an existing login method or replace a real sporting account merely to manufacture coverage. Development previews are shared through Tailscale; the registered canonical test OAuth origin/callback stays unchanged. A Hermes loopback callback stays private to the intended machine or an exact SSH port forward.

The installed **AimHarder MCP Pilot** connector was also exercised through native Codex Apps, a separate channel from ChatGPT's browser conversation and the Hermes CLI. These calls do not fill the remaining browser ChatGPT/Hermes family rows above.

`get_personal_activity` for the single date 2026-10-19 failed twice with the application's safe `INVALID_ACTIVITY_RESPONSE`. A one-day counterprobe for 2026-09-19 on the same linked account/gym then returned a typed response with complete requested-date coverage. The outer connector's `INVALID_ARGUMENT` classification is not an application invalid-date finding. A later single installed-Hermes observation identified calendar structural rejection at `workouts.<date>.TIPOWODs.<unknown-key>`; no actual property name/value or meaning was retained. Temporary diagnostics were retired and the original production bundle restored. The [calendar compatibility candidate](validation.md#opaque-calendar-metadata-candidate-2026-10-19) now treats only that required, unprojected object's keys as opaque, preserving rates references, strict control metadata, month/duplicate and independent detail owner/gym/date/coverage checks. Actual corrected deployment/October recovery remains pending. Keep this defect and the separate natural-expiry refresh failure distinct from the resolved JWKS issue. No real sporting write was made in these checks.

For each observed journey retain only sanitized timestamp, deployed source/version, client surface/version, outcome and necessary counts. Do not persist callback codes, tokens, token stores, cookies, email addresses, private booking/activity results or provider payloads in the repository/tracker.
