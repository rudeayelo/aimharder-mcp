## Observer and privacy boundary

The exact [observer source](hosted-telemetry-cpu-observer.mjs) accepts only the named isolated producer, its stateless execution model and the existing `*/5 * * * *` schedule. It explicitly projects the runtime invocation timestamp, nonnegative integer CPU/wall milliseconds and a closed outcome. It never serializes an original trace, request, logs, exceptions, headers, participant identifiers or sporting data. Non-scheduled events produce no output. Missing/malformed metrics are discarded rather than becoming zero; a genuine integer zero remains zero at this resolution.

The observer has no public origin, protected secret, storage binding, outbound fetch or persistent logging. Its sole variable is an absolute capture deadline; its CPU limit is 100 ms. An ephemeral real-time tail on the observer transports only projected console packets. The local receiver rejects additional keys and unknown values, discards original trace envelopes and retains technical packets only. It records stream closure, malformed packets and sampling/drop indications. It does not infer units from raw producer `trace-v1` fields. [Real-time tails do not store Workers Logs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/observability/logs/real-time-logs/); persistent Logs/Traces/Issues remain disabled on the producer and observer.

[Tail Workers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/observability/logs/tail-workers/) are available on Paid and billed for their own CPU. The observer's consumption is separate from the producer CPU sample; this capture does not establish a complete invoice or sustained-cost estimate. The producer has no explicit `limits` override in its native settings, and the test preserves that state. The [current documented Paid CPU limit](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/#cpu-time) for a cron interval shorter than one hour is 30 seconds; this is a plan reference, not a measured capacity guarantee. The earlier nominal 10 ms Free reference remains historical and cannot establish suitability after a Paid run.

The [eight local contract/privacy checks](hosted-telemetry-cpu-observer.test.mjs) cover scheduled projection, hostile HTTP exclusion, other producers/object traces, other schedules/alarms, malformed times, static unknown outcomes, expiry and invocation-timestamp validity. Run `node --test docs/hosted-telemetry-cpu-observer.test.mjs`. The native gate remains separate from these fixtures.
