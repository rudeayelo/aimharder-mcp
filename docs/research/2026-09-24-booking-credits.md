# Booking credit balance: read-only endpoint research (2026-09-24)

## Question and result

Can the authenticated account holder read the number of booking credits still available for the current month at 9NBC through an AimHarder endpoint? **No endpoint or response field has yet been verified to provide that value.** This is an open contract, not evidence that AimHarder lacks such a function.

The account holder reports that a confirmed 9NBC booking occupies one credit and that a timely cancellation makes that credit available again. This is user experience, not a verified API balance contract. A booking count must not be subtracted from a presumed monthly allowance to manufacture a remaining balance: tariffs can have different eligible class groups, periods, limits, top-ups, and late-cancellation consequences.

The account holder clarified that their 9NBC subscription grants 15 sessions per calendar month and unused credits expire on the first day of the next month. Session packs may have a longer validity, possibly about six months, but that duration is uncertain and may vary by gym. This explains the requested "current month" view for their subscription; it does not define a universal AimHarder credit period or an aggregate balance across subscription and packs.

The account holder also explicitly confirms that their own account can book Open Box, despite the monthly tariff's public description mentioning guided sessions only. Treat this as account-specific user evidence; it does not establish eligibility for every holder of that tariff or replace a fresh preflight for a particular class.

## Authenticated own-account comparison

After the account holder authorized local 1Password CLI access, a read-only check used the existing AimHarder credential inside a subprocess. The login response's `data.auth.authOK`, its authentication cookie, `/api/whoami` account identity, and the 9NBC `client` membership were verified before gym queries. Credential values, cookies, account IDs, and private response bodies were not printed or saved.

The complete key structures of `GET /api/whoami`, `GET /api/nextBookings?box=<verified-boid>`, and `GET /api/bookings?box=<verified-boid>&day=20260923` and `day=20260925` were inspected for credit, balance, tariff, pricing, allowance, and period fields. The two daily schedule responses contained 19 and 14 class rows respectively. No matching balance or period key appeared in those inspected responses. This confirms only that these known read views do not expose an identifiable remaining-credit field for the sampled account and dates; it does not cover other authenticated endpoints or infer a zero balance. The live reads were not MCP balance-tool validation because no such tool exists.

Existing authenticated research verified `GET /api/whoami`, `GET /api/bookings`, and `GET /api/nextBookings` for account discovery, schedule, and booking views, respectively. Those investigations did not validate a credit-balance field or an exhaustive tariff/entitlement view; see [account discovery](../api-research.md#account-login-and-gym-selection-2026-09-21), [class schedules](../api-research.md#class-schedules-2026-09-22), and [upcoming bookings](../api-research.md#upcoming-bookings-2026-09-22). Their projections cannot be treated as a credit ledger.

## Verification boundary

No authenticated tariff or credit-balance endpoint was found in the inspected frontend sources, so none was called. The known own-account views above were queried, but they do not establish an exhaustive list of authenticated or mobile-app API calls. This investigation did not call any booking/cancellation endpoint, inspect another member, or copy private API responses into this file. No unofficial implementation has supplied a verified credit-balance contract for this account.

Before implementing a balance query, inspect the authenticated **own-account** tariff/booking screen and its network calls through an authorized session. For any candidate read-only endpoint, compare a minimal, sanitized response with the balance shown in AimHarder before and after a user-approved booking and timely cancellation. Verify whether it reports *available*, *used*, *reserved*, or *total* credits; which tariff and class types it covers; and the actual start/end dates and zone of its credit period. The account holder's “current month” may mean a calendar month or an active tariff cycle, and the API must establish which one. Handle unlimited plans and multiple simultaneous entitlements without reducing them to a single invented number. Continue to report credit recovery as unverified until a balance read confirms it.
